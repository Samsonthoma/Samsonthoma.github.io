06 Technical notes

Prompt Engineering for Repeatable Results

When prompts include constraints, role, expected format, and edge-case handling, output quality becomes consistent. I use a reusable evaluation rubric that rates correctness, brevity, and safety before production use.

Vibe Coding with Guardrails

Vibe coding works best when fast ideation is paired with boundaries: define acceptance criteria first, lock scope, and run quick validation loops. Creativity increases when quality checks are built in from the start.

Reliable Web Deployment in Small Teams

For school and education websites, deployment reliability matters more than complexity. Clear rollback steps, static asset caching, and basic structured data often drive better uptime and stronger search visibility.
